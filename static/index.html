Before you try it
The business is fictional

“Luxe Hair Studio” and its information are sample data created for demonstration purposes.

Bookings are handled by the AI

There is no direct booking form. The assistant checks the business Google Calendar before confirming an appointment.

Required details

To book, the assistant needs your name, email, phone number, service and preferred date and time.

What to test

Ask about services, prices and hours, then say “I'd like to book a women's haircut tomorrow afternoon.”
