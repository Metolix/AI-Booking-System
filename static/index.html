This is a demonstration website. You're testing a sample AI customer-support and appointment-booking assistant for a fictional business.
Before you try it
The business is fictional

“Luxe Hair Studio” and its information are sample data created for demonstration purposes.

Bookings are simulated

Appointments use a demo booking database. If Google Calendar is configured by the owner, confirmed appointments are also written to that calendar.

AI-generated responses

The assistant can make mistakes. Never enter passwords, payment details, or confidential information.

Anti-abuse controls are enabled

The demo limits requests, prevents duplicate/overlapping appointments, limits upcoming bookings per customer/network, and validates every booking server-side.

What to test

Ask about services, prices and hours, then try “I'd like to book a women's haircut tomorrow afternoon.” The assistant will check live availability before booking.
