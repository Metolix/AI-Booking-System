Before you try it
What you're seeing

This demo shows how an AI support assistant can answer customer questions using a business-specific knowledge base.

The business is fictional

“Luxe Hair Studio” and its information are sample data created for demonstration purposes. Nothing here represents a real business.

Responses are AI-generated

The assistant can make mistakes, misunderstand questions, or provide incomplete answers. A production system would be tested and monitored before handling real customers.

Don't enter sensitive information

Please don't enter passwords, payment details, private customer information, or anything confidential. This is not a production customer-support channel.

This is not connected to your business

The demo does not access your website, CRM, inbox, orders, bookings, customer database, or internal systems. Those integrations can be added to a production deployment where appropriate.

What a real deployment can include

A production version can be trained on your approved business information, branded to match your website, connected to relevant tools, given escalation rules, and configured around your support workflow.

What to test

Try common customer questions, unusual questions, follow-ups, pricing questions, policy questions, and questions the assistant should not be able to answer. The goal is to see how the experience feels—not to treat every answer as authoritative.

Important

This demo is intended for evaluation only. Any production implementation should use appropriate security, privacy, data-retention, access-control, monitoring, and human-escalation practices.
